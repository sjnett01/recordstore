RecordStore preview scratch transition

Place your chosen scratch sound here as:
    scratch.wav

MP3 is also supported if you set app.scratch_fx_path in config.php, for example:
    'scratch_fx_path' => 'private/assets/scratch.mp3',

The preview builder trims the effect to 0.75 seconds and inserts it between preview sections 1/2 and 2/3.
The three music sections are shortened to 29.5 seconds so the finished preview remains approximately 90 seconds.
Existing previews are unchanged; regenerate/re-upload a track to bake the transition into its preview.
